How The Charm Bar Works 💖

  1. 1. Choose your piece

    Switch between Bracelet, Necklace, Rings, or Earrings using the top buttons above the velvet box.

  2. 2. Pick genuine pearls & charms

    Browse glowing South Sea pearls, Akoya drops, Tangled charms, and heirloom rings in the tray. Tap any piece to place it.

  3. 3. Select metal & size

    Switch between 18K Corona Gold or Sterling Silver, and select your preferred size (16cm, 18cm, 20cm).

  4. 4. Box it up!

    Click "BOX IT UP" on the pink cash register to open the Gift Studio: choose a wrap, write a card, and watch your jewelry get packed and sealed!
